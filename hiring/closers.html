About New Visions

New Visions is a Moroccan marketing agency helping small and medium businesses grow through strategic digital marketing solutions.

  • Advertising (Meta & TikTok)
  • Funnels & Websites
  • Content & Automation

We work with real clients and real budgets. Our sales team is a key part of our growth.

Your Role

As a Sales Closer, you will:

  • Talk with leads from WhatsApp and calls
  • Qualify potential clients
  • Present our services clearly
  • Follow a proven sales script
  • Close deals and earn commission
  • Update simple CRM records
  • Work with a Sales Lead who trains and supports you

What You Will Learn

This is training + income, not just a job.

How to talk to business owners

How to handle objections

How to sell with trust (not pressure)

How to close deals ethically

How to grow in income and responsibility

Requirements

You must meet these criteria

  • Speak Moroccan Darija and French (English is a plus)
  • Be confident on phone and WhatsApp
  • Be available full-time or serious part-time
  • Accept feedback and coaching
  • Be organized and disciplined
  • Have internet + phone/computer

No diploma required. Personality and discipline matter more than experience.
